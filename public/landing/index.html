Ask Play by VoiceThe essentials
What is Play by Voice?
Play by Voice

A hackathon project exploring games through speaking and listening. Its companion can describe a chessboard, help you consider a move, and handle game controls when you ask.

Which games can I try?
Play by Voice

Chess is playable, against the computer or in local two-player mode. Loups-Garous is planned. The app also previews Tic-Tac-Toe, which is not yet playable.

Do I need to see the screen?
Play by Voice

The companion supports spoken navigation, board descriptions, and moves. Starting voice requires an initial action and microphone permission. Some preferences still use on-screen or keyboard controls. Full assistive-technology usability testing is still ahead.

Do I need a microphone or an API key?
Play by Voice

Live voice needs a microphone and a server configured with an OpenAI API key. Players never enter a key on this page. The board, keyboard controls, and typed commands also work without live voice. This landing page never requests microphone access.

Can I ask for help with a move?
Play by Voice

Yes. Ask for a hint, a description of the board, or a suggested move. A suggested move is a proposal; it still needs your confirmation. You can also ask for a quick guide.

What happens to my voice?
Play by Voice

This landing page does not capture audio. When you start live voice in the game, microphone audio is sent to OpenAI for the conversation. The app does not store raw audio or transcripts in local storage; it saves game position and preferences on your device. Use “End voice” to close the session. See the project documentation for setup and current limitations.
